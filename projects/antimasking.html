AntiMasking

Overview

Made during the 2026 Global Game Jam in 48 hours, AntiMasking is a chaotic first-person auto-runner where you play a deranged individual sprinting through a grocery store and ripping masks off shoppers. I was heavily involved in the early brainstorming phase, focusing on keeping whatever direction we chose coherent and technically achievable within the deadline. Given how chaotic the final concept ended up, the ideas we cut were even more over the top.

What I did

  • Contributed during the early brainstorming phase of a 48-hour Global Game Jam project with a ten-person team.
  • Helped keep the concept coherent and technically achievable within the jam constraints.
  • Connected ideas together, flagged scope and design issues, and helped narrow the team toward a realistic direction.
  • Helped troubleshoot level whiteboxing issues and adjusted turning triggers for the auto-running character's corner movement.
  • Made light documentation updates by adding references and clarifying information to keep the team aligned.
