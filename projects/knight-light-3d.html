Knight Light (3D)

Overview

A 3D third-person action-adventure built in Unreal (unrelated to my 2D KnightLight). Another designer and I joined an existing senior team of around 25 to help push the project forward, and I was brought on primarily for combat design and UX. The build was rough when I arrived, so I started by playing it and documenting everything that felt inconsistent or unclear.

From there I created and maintained a large Design Requests spreadsheet that broke work into actionable, cross-discipline tasks with context and reasoning attached — an important communication bridge on a team that size — and wrote combat and enemy design docs while tuning variables and rebalancing the wave system directly in Unreal.

What I did

  • Joined an existing senior team and helped continue development on a 3D third-person action-adventure game in Unreal.
  • Brought in primarily for combat design and UX work.
  • Played the latest build and documented what worked, what needed improvement, and what felt inconsistent or unclear.
  • Created a large feedback document to identify key problem areas from a UX and gameplay perspective.
  • Built and maintained a large Design Requests spreadsheet that broke down actionable tasks across programming, art, audio, and VFX.
  • Added context, reasoning, and implementation notes to requests so team members understood both the problem and the intended outcome.
  • Wrote design documents proposing improvements to player combat, enemy behavior, and new enemy concepts.
  • Tuned player and enemy variables and helped rebalance the wave system in Unreal.
  • Contributed to a large team of around 25 people by strengthening cohesion and production clarity.
