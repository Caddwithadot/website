Project Nasu

Overview

Project Nasu was my first team project at DigiPen — a 2D action-puzzle platformer built entirely in Unity with Visual Scripting, on a team of three designers. Coming from a technical school background, I stepped into a leadership role: setting up the project, onboarding the team to source control, establishing workflow, and running SCRUM. Since the others were still learning Unity, I took on most of the implementation and effectively acted as lead programmer.

I designed and built the player controller, collectibles, hazards, enemies, and a speedrun timer — including a flexible, readable set of hazard tools (three laser types) and layered enemy behaviors like a patrolling rat with a respawner and a mutated hamster that combined patrol movement with timed laser attacks.

What I did

  • Served on a three-designer team and naturally stepped into a leadership role after coming from a technical school background.
  • Set up the Unity project, introduced the team to source control, helped establish workflow, and ran SCRUM meetings to keep production on track.
  • Took on most of the implementation work and effectively acted as the lead programmer while still contributing to design.
  • Designed, prototyped, and implemented the player controller, collectibles, hazards, enemies, and a speedrun timer with time tracking.
  • Built readable hazard tools for level design, including three laser types: a red laser disabled by shooting a level button, a green laser that blinked on an interval, and a blue laser that rotated within a 90-degree range.
  • Implemented enemy behaviors including a rat that patrolled between two points, a rat spawner that respawned rats after death, and a mutated hamster that combined patrol behavior with laser attacks.
  • Gained first-hand experience fully owning both design and implementation on a team.
