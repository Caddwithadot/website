BLOOPS

Overview

Made during the 2025 Global Game Jam in 96 hours, BLOOPS is a 3D top-down puzzle game where players redirect bubbles from a soap dispenser to a drain using objects that alter each bubble's trajectory. It was my first game jam, on a nine-person multidisciplinary team that included artists from DigiPen's Spain campus.

Knowing those artists had seen their jam work go unimplemented before, I made integration a personal goal — handling most of the 3D gameplay asset implementation, connecting art to systems, and helping a programmer new to Unity find their footing, while designing the trajectory-altering mechanics that structured the puzzles.

What I did

  • Completed a 96-hour Global Game Jam project on a nine-person multidisciplinary team.
  • Helped guide the team toward a shared concept during brainstorming after the theme reveal.
  • Assisted one programmer who was new to Unity by helping them navigate the engine and troubleshoot issues.
  • Handled most of the 3D gameplay asset implementation, connecting art assets to systems and ensuring prefabs worked correctly in-game.
  • Designed the mechanics that changed bubble trajectory and structured the puzzle flow.
  • Implemented the camera perspective swap toggle, randomized floor tile generator, counter prop spawner, and an early inventory prototype.
  • Focused on making sure the core mechanics worked and the team stayed aligned within a tight deadline.
