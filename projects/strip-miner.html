Strip Miner

Overview

Strip Miner was a summer Unity project with a small team of three, built around the simple, sticky fantasy of strip mining. The player starts with a weak tool and gradually upgrades it by mining and depositing voxels for credits, digging faster and pushing deeper over time. The programmer built the voxel foundation while I focused on gameplay prototyping and system design.

I wrote the full game design document — upgrade systems, quota progression, helper items, and a roguelike layer structure — and tuned pacing so early progression felt meaningful and later play delivered a real power-fantasy escalation.

What I did

  • Worked on a small three-person summer project built around the fantasy of strip mining in a voxel-based world.
  • Focused on gameplay prototyping and system design while the programmer built the voxel foundation.
  • Implemented and tested the early mining loop, deposit flow, and upgrade structure.
  • Wrote the full game design document covering upgrade systems, quota progression, helper items, and a roguelike layer structure.
  • Designed the core loop around mining ore, depositing it for credits, upgrading tools, and pushing deeper into underground layers.
  • Focused on pacing and motivation, including slow early progression and later power fantasy escalation.
  • Designed systems such as quota requirements, helper tools, and depth-based unlocks to reinforce forward momentum.
