AGP — Alien Game Project

Multiplayer Extraction Prototype · Unity
Summer 2024Systems Designer & Gameplay ProgrammerTeam of 2
AGP

Overview

AGP, short for Alien Game Project, was a summer attempt at a Lethal Company-style co-op prototype with one other designer. We shelved it midway, but it pushed me further technically than anything before it — my first real crack at multiplayer gameplay rather than just networked movement, learning synchronization, shared state, and server authority the hard way.

I implemented a physics-based networked controller with an interaction/inventory system, adopted ScriptableObjects for item data, and built a node-based procedural dungeon generator that stitched room prefabs together with seed-based rules, synchronized drop-pod deployment, and ship extraction for all players.

What I did

  • Helped build a Lethal Company-style co-op prototype over the summer with one other designer.
  • Gained first serious experience with multiplayer gameplay architecture, including synchronization, shared game states, and server authority decisions.
  • Implemented a physics-based player controller and a networked interaction/inventory system for picking up and dropping items.
  • Learned Scriptable Object workflows and used them to define item data.
  • Built a node-based procedural dungeon generator that connected room prefabs through doorways and randomly selected and rotated rooms during expansion.
  • Designed rules for drop-off zones, including guaranteed spacing and a seed-based active insertion point.
  • Implemented drop-pod deployment and ship extraction sequences synchronized for all players.
  • Helped explore a larger refuge-and-escape concept centered on deploying, looting, and extracting before the ship fled again.
