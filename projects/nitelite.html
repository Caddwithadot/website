NiteLite

Overview

NiteLite was a custom 3D engine written entirely in C++ with a custom C# scripting layer, built by the same team behind the 2D engine used for KnightLight. Another designer and I used the scripting layer to build gameplay demos that showcased its functionality, and a large part of my time went to playtesting engine features, reporting bugs, and documenting usability issues from a designer's perspective.

For the final demo I built the player navigation and scene transition system, including a non-Euclidean door transition achieved by controlling scene loads and teleporting the player while the camera was fully obscured, plus a standardized showcase room template the rest of the team used to present their prototypes.

What I did

  • Worked on a custom 3D C++ engine with a custom C# scripting layer alongside another designer.
  • Spent substantial time playtesting engine features, reporting bugs, documenting usability issues, and suggesting missing workflow improvements from a designer's perspective.
  • Built the player navigation and scene transition system for the final engine demo.
  • Implemented a basic first-person controller and interactive doors that connected prototype showcase scenes.
  • Created a non-Euclidean transition effect by controlling scene loads and teleporting the player while the camera was obscured.
  • Built a standardized showcase room template with a viewing window and interaction point to present prototypes in a consistent format.
  • Contributed design-focused usability feedback that improved the engine workflow for the rest of the team.
