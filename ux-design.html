How I approach it

Most of my UX work is about clarity rather than interface. I care about whether the player understands what just happened and what they can do next, and my usual instinct is to fix that in the mechanic or the feedback rather than adding text explaining it.

A lot of that comes from running playtests and watching where people get confused. When testers kept misunderstanding a system on KnightLight, the fix was not a tutorial popup, it was iterating on the visual and audio feedback until the mechanic explained itself.
