Forgeborne

Forgeborne

First-Person Action Prototype · Unity · Academic

Forgeborne was a five-week solo prototype for DigiPen's DES365 Game Feel course — a first-person action experience built around a single unifying mechanic: electromagnetism. The player uses metal-manipulating tech to forge and test adaptive weapons in a junkyard arena. The brief wanted three weapons with three attacks each; my goal was to tie all nine together through one systemic interaction rather than nine isolated kits.

About

I'm Taylor, a Technical Game Designer finishing my BA at DigiPen Institute of Technology, with roughly six years of hands-on game design and development experience. My skill set sits at the intersection of design and implementation — I care most about designing mechanics, building tools, and implementing features directly in-engine, taking ideas from concept all the way through iteration.

I'm a logical, systems-oriented problem solver with a strong focus on polish and player clarity. I'm comfortable making design tradeoffs around scope and technical constraints, and willing to simplify, refactor, or cut features when it improves the experience. I've served as Design Lead on multiple team projects, run SCRUM as a de facto lead, and collaborated closely with programmers, artists, and audio designers. I'm strongest in Unity, with additional experience in Unreal and custom C++ engines, programming primarily in C#.
