About

Technical Game Designer · Redmond, WA · Open to remote

I'm Taylor, a Technical Game Designer finishing my BA at DigiPen Institute of Technology, with about six years of hands-on game design and development experience. Before DigiPen I went to a technical school called Sno-Isle TECH during my last two years of high school, which is what started all of this. My skill set sits somewhere between design and implementation. I've worked in UX, systems, and technical design, but what I enjoy most is designing mechanics, building tools, and putting features into the engine myself, taking an idea from a rough concept all the way through iteration.

I'm a logical, systems-oriented problem solver, and I care a lot about polish and about whether things are clear to the player. I'm comfortable making tradeoffs around scope and technical limits, and I'm fine simplifying or cutting a feature when it makes the game better. I've been Design Lead on a few team projects, run SCRUM meetings to keep production on track, and worked closely with programmers, artists, and audio designers. I'm strongest in Unity, and I've also worked in Unreal and in two custom C++ engines. I program mostly in C#. I'm not a full computer science programmer, but I'm comfortable enough to build my own designs and keep iterating quickly.
